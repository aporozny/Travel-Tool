Drift: Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Drift is operated by QuantMS ("we", "us"). Contact: aporozny@gmail.com.

Part 1: Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name or display name, and your sign-in details.
  • Trip and preference data: destinations, saved places, trips you create and the interests you choose.
  • Location: your approximate or precise location, only when you allow it on your device, to show nearby places and to run safety features.
  • Safety information: emergency contacts you add, check-in times, and records of safety alerts and Safety Line calls.
  • Technical data: device type, app version and error logs used to keep the service working.

2. How we use it

We use your data to run Drift: to show places and recommendations, to save your trips, to send check-in and safety messages, to contact your emergency contacts when a check-in is missed, to handle Safety Line calls, and to fix problems and keep the service secure.

3. Sharing

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with anyone for their own purposes, including advertisers. Where we need other companies to run parts of the service, they process data only on our behalf and only to provide that service. These include providers of maps and places data, email and SMS delivery, voice calls and hosting. We may also disclose data if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety in an emergency.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your data until your account is deleted. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data. Short-lived technical logs and backups are removed on a routine schedule after that.

5. Your choices and rights

  • You can turn location access off in your device settings at any time. Some features will stop working.
  • You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it or delete your account by emailing aporozny@gmail.com.
  • If you are in Australia and think we have mishandled your information, contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

6. Security

We protect data with access controls and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

Drift is not intended for people under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.
