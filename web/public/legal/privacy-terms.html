Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 November 2026 · drifttravel.app

1. Who we are

Drift ("Drift," "we," "us") is operated by QuantMS and runs drifttravel.app, a travel-community platform connecting independent travelers, helping them discover places through member recommendations rather than paid placement, and offering safety tools including check-ins and an emergency voice line.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have, including for the more sensitive categories: safety-contact information, emergency call transcripts and live location data.

In short: we do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone for their own purposes, including advertisers. The companies that help us run Drift (listed in Section 5) process data only on our behalf. The one exception is the Meta Pixel on our website, described in Section 3.

2. What we collect

Account information

Email address, phone number, and password (stored as a salted hash, never in plain text) when you register.

Profile information

Name, home city and country, avatar, display name, and, if you complete onboarding, your travel preferences: budget range, activity interests, travel pace, adrenaline level, social preferences, and what you're looking to get out of the community (meeting members, finding travel buddies, sharing trip reports).

Trip and safety information

Trips you plan or share, including destination, dates and notes; safety contacts you add (their name, email, phone and relationship to you); and check-in schedules you set for a trip. See Section 7.

Location data

Only ever collected with your explicit, revocable consent. See Section 6 for the full detail on Trip Mode, which is the feature that generates this data.

Safety Line data

If you call Drift's emergency voice line, we keep a text transcript of the call (not the raw audio), the call outcome, and technical records of the call. See Section 8.

Booking and payment information

When you book a flight through Drift, your name, contact details and travel details are shared with our booking partner (Duffel) and the relevant airline to complete the booking. Card payment details are entered directly into our payment partner's own secure form and never pass through or get stored on Drift's own servers.

Community content

Posts, reviews, messages to other members, and connection requests you make are stored and, depending on your settings, visible to other members or the public.

Technical data

Device and browser type, app version, IP address and error logs, used to keep the service working and secure.

3. Cookies, local storage and the Meta Pixel

Drift uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in. Your session and refresh tokens are stored this way. This is not used for advertising.

Meta Pixel. Our website loads the Meta (Facebook) Pixel for every visitor, whether or not you have an account, so that we can measure the results of our advertising. When a page loads, the Pixel sends Meta a page-view event along with technical information your browser provides, such as your IP address, browser details and the page you visited, and Meta may set or read its own cookies. We do not send your name, email, phone number or other account details through the Pixel. Meta handles that information under its own privacy policy and acts as an independent party for its own purposes. This is the one place where data about your visit reaches a company that may use it for itself.

You can limit it by blocking third-party trackers in your browser, using a tracking-protection extension, or adjusting your ad preferences in your Meta account. We do not currently show a cookie banner. If you would like to object, contact us (Section 18).

We do not use any other advertising or cross-site tracking tools on the Drift website. If we add analytics tools later, we will update this section first.

4. How we use it

  • To operate your account and the core features you use (Explore, Trips, Messages, Community)
  • To match you with places, trips and other travelers based on your stated preferences, never based on who paid for placement
  • To operate the Safety Line, Trip Mode and Trip Check-In safety features described below
  • To process flight bookings through our booking partner
  • To send you service notifications (booking confirmations, safety alerts you've opted into, account security)
  • To measure our advertising, as described in Section 3
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform, including responding to reports of harassment, fraud or safety concerns

We do not sell your personal information. We do not accept payment from any operator, business or advertiser to influence what's shown to you in Explore or search results. See also the platform-neutrality commitment in our Terms of Service.

A note on your safety contacts

Your safety contacts are usually people, not Drift accounts: a parent, partner or friend who may never sign up for Drift themselves. When you add someone as a safety contact, you're telling us it's okay to send them your name and, during an active alert, your location. Depending on the permissions you set for that contact, they may also be notified if you trigger an SOS or miss a scheduled check-in. You're responsible for having that person's okay before adding them. If a safety contact wants their information removed, they (or you, on their behalf) can contact us. See Section 18.

6. Location and Trip Mode

Trip Mode is a fully opt-in feature. It is off by default for every member. Turning it on requires an explicit, separate action. It is not bundled with account creation or any other consent.

  • What's collected: your device's location, reported periodically (roughly every 5 minutes or 250 metres moved) while Trip Mode is on and the app is open in your browser.
  • Where it goes: your most recent point is kept briefly to power two features (below), and a history of points is stored. See Section 9 for how long.
  • Who can see it:
    • The Drift Safety Line AI, as a starting hint (not a fact) if you call. See Section 8.
    • Other members who also have Trip Mode on, if they're within the distance they search, shown only as an approximate distance (rounded to the nearest kilometre), never an exact point.
  • Turning it off: immediately removes your live location from both features above. It does not wait for anything to expire.
  • An important limitation, stated honestly: Trip Mode only works while Drift is open in your browser tab. It is not, and today cannot be, "always on" in the background after you close the tab or lock your phone. That would require a native mobile app, which does not yet exist. This applies to Drift generally: Drift is currently a web application, not a native iOS or Android app, which also affects how reliably push-style notifications and Safety Line callbacks reach you if the tab isn't open.

7. Trip Check-In

Separately from Trip Mode, you can declare a trip and set a check-in interval (for example, every 24 hours). If you don't check in within that window, the trip is marked "overdue," which can lead to your safety contacts or a Drift reviewer being notified. This is the same underlying process as a manual SOS or a Safety Line call, just triggered by your not checking in rather than an active request for help.

Checking in can include your location if you choose to share it at that moment. The interval and status are visible only to you unless a notification is actually triggered.

8. Safety Line

The Safety Line is Drift's inbound emergency voice line, answered first by an AI agent. Here's what happens if you call it, and what the AI is built to do and not do:

  • The AI always identifies itself as an AI, never claims to be a person, and never claims to have connected you to a human reviewer unless that connection has actually happened.
  • If you indicate a life-threatening emergency, the AI's first instruction is to tell you to contact your local emergency services, before anything else.
  • The AI will always confirm your country before giving you a local emergency number, rather than guess, even if a location hint is available.
  • We keep a text transcript of the call, deliberately not the raw audio. That transcript, and any location hint associated with the call, is made available to the human Drift reviewer responsible for following up on your case. During the call, the voice and AI providers listed in Section 5 process your audio in real time under their own terms.
  • Every Safety Line call is reviewed and closed out by a human. The AI itself can never mark a case resolved. Human review timing is not guaranteed to be immediate, but review always happens.
  • The AI will attempt to connect you live to a human reviewer during your call if the situation warrants it. If a live connection can't be made in that moment, it will tell you a reviewer will follow up after the call, rather than claim you're connected when you're not.
  • If you have Trip Mode on, the Safety Line may use your most recent location ping as a starting hint. This hint can be outdated if you've moved since your last update, including because Trip Mode only updates while your Drift tab is open. The AI is instructed to verbally confirm your location rather than rely on the hint alone.

The Safety Line is not a replacement for calling your local emergency services. It's designed to help you reach the right number fast and get a human involved, not to replace 000, 911, 112 or similar.

10. Your rights and choices

  • You can review and update your profile and preferences directly in the app at any time.
  • You can turn Trip Mode off at any time, with immediate effect on the live-matching layer.
  • You can delete your safety contacts at any time.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account by emailing us. We handle these requests by hand.
  • If you are in Australia and believe we have mishandled your personal information, contact us first. You can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

11. Communications

We send transactional and safety messages (booking confirmations, SOS and check-in alerts, account security). These aren't optional while you're using the features that generate them. You can't opt out of an SOS alert reaching your safety contact, for example, without turning off that feature. If we ever introduce marketing messages, they will come with a clear opt-out.

12. Children's privacy

Drift is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16 years old. This age matches the eligibility age in Terms of Service Section 3.

13. International data transfers

Drift is a global product. Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States, where several of our service providers (including the AI and voice providers behind the Safety Line) are based.

14. Security

We use measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and access controls limiting who inside Drift can see safety-related data. No system is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you and the regulator where the law requires.

15. Identity verification (planned)

We're building an optional identity verification feature to help members trust who they're meeting. It is not active yet. When it goes live, we will update this policy before it processes any identity documents, describing exactly what's collected, how long it's kept, and how to have it deleted.

16. How we score operators

Businesses listed on Drift (operators) are shown a trust score and badge based on signals including identity verification, review history, responsiveness and how long they've been on the platform. This uses reviews and interactions members provide, but doesn't affect what data we collect from you as a traveler beyond what's already described above.
